Road requirement (JSON)

Paste a road-illuminate requirement set as is, or wrap it as "checks" with vehicle settings. Check types: point, range, width, zone, with op and limit; y is positive to the left, as in road-illuminate. Optional z per check sets its evaluation height. Optional class (B, CS, DS, ES; driving beam BS, DSES). Optional targets: imax, flux_lm, spreadH_deg, spreadV_deg.
